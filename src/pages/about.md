I'm a software developer. I reside in Seattle, WA.

I'm happiest moving at a comfortable walking pace. I enjoy walking around Seattle's parks and neighborhoods as well as going on slow hikes. My coworkers often find me on my walking pad.

I formerly worked <nobr>8 years</nobr> as a licensed structural engineer and specialized in steel connections and stair design. I worked on schools, the Charlotte Convention Center, and the Marriott Marquis Houston with a Texas-shaped lazy river.
